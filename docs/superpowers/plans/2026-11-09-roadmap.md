# Stagepack Studio: roadmap

The design (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-native-slidev-editor-design.md`) covers several subsystems in four repos, so it is built as a series of plans. Each plan ends with software that runs and can be tested on its own.

## Plans

### 1. Walking skeleton (Linux)

Open a `.sptx` in the native window, select and move things on the slide, see and change the selection in a native inspector, and save back into the `.sptx`.

This plan builds the three pieces everything else stands on:
- the bridge protocol;
- Stagepack's `serve` sidecar with whole-folder save;
- loading Studio without touching the talk.

File: `2026-11-09-plan-1-walking-skeleton.md`.

### 2. Windows check, slides, notes, undo and recovery

1. **Before anything else,** run the plan 1 build on a real Windows machine:
   - the sidecar, including stopping on stdin EOF under the job object;
   - the shell with WebView2 and `GPUI_DISABLE_DIRECT_COMPOSITION`.
2. **Then:**
   - the slide strip with live thumbnails: new from a layout, duplicate, delete, reorder, and hide or unhide from a slide list read from the source;
   - the notes panel;
   - Save As;
   - undo across the session, from snapshots of the talk's files;
   - AutoRecover: folders holding unsaved edits are kept, and the next open offers them back;
   - edits to slides imported with `src:` are saved too.

### 3. Text

- Styled inline spans round-trip in the fork's in-place editor.
- The Home tab:
  - bold, italic and underline;
  - size;
  - colour, from the theme's palette;
  - alignment;
  - bullets and numbering.
- Copy and paste of elements and slides, also between two open talks.

### 4. Insert

- **Pictures:** from a file, by dragging in, and by pasting.
- **Shapes:** the shapes addon, a new npm package.
- **Icons:** bundled Iconify sets, with a picker.
- **Tables:** a grid.
- **Components:** the palette, natively.

### 5. Arrange, animate and layout

- Align, distribute and order.
- The Animate tab: click steps, entrances, stagger, motion, transitions and the scrubber.
- The layout picker.

### 6. Present and export

- *Present* from the editor.
- PowerPoint export (editable and with animations) and PDF export.
- In slidev-pptx-export: the shapes addon becomes native PowerPoint shapes.

### 7. New presentation, packaging and install

- Starter templates.
- The editor runtime as a second runtime input in Stagepack's build, with licences.
- **Installers:**
  - Linux: installer, `.sptx` default, and taking the default back.
  - Windows: installer, WebView2 check, the popup workaround, and the licences screen.
- **Stagepack's release:** it bundles the shapes addon and allows it in the trust check.
- CI on Linux and Windows.

## Order

Plans 2 to 6 each depend on plan 1. After plan 2, plans 3, 4 and 5 can be built in any order. Plan 6 needs plan 4's shapes addon, and plan 7 comes last.
